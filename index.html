Part A Airport Infrastructure & Environment

Scores the physical environment of the airport. This sets the base risk level that Part B and C then modify.

Part B Service Provider Capability

Scores each service provider's capability. Acts as a multiplier on Part A — a strong SP dampens risk (×<1), a weak SP amplifies it (×>1). Add an entry for each SP serving the station.

Part C System Integration Assessment

Scores how well WestJet, the airport, and the SP work together as a system. Acts as a second multiplier on A×B. Must be re-reviewed whenever Part B changes (new SP).
